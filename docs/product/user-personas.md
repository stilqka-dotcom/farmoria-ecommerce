# User Personas

---

# Overview

Farmoria is designed for customers who value healthy nutrition, high-quality products and a simple online shopping experience.

Understanding the needs, goals and frustrations of different customer groups helps prioritize product features and improve usability.

This document defines the primary user personas for Version 1.0.

---

# Persona 1 – Health-Conscious Parent

## Profile

- Age: 30–45
- Occupation: Full-time employee
- Family: Married with children
- Digital Skills: Intermediate

## Goals

- Buy healthy food for the family
- Save time while shopping
- Find trustworthy products
- Compare nutritional information

## Needs

- Easy navigation
- Clear product categories
- Detailed product descriptions
- Fast checkout
- Mobile-friendly shopping

## Pain Points

- Poor website organization
- Missing product information
- Slow loading pages
- Difficult checkout process

## Buying Motivation

- Healthier lifestyle
- Better nutrition for children
- Convenience

## Important Features

- Product categories
- Search
- Filters
- Product images
- Nutrition information
- Shopping cart
- Secure checkout

# Persona 3 – Natural Cosmetics Buyer

## Profile

- Age: 25–50
- Occupation: Various
- Shopping Frequency: Monthly
- Digital Skills: Intermediate

## Goals

- Discover natural cosmetic products
- Read detailed descriptions
- Trust product quality
- Compare alternatives

## Needs

- High-quality images
- Product descriptions
- Ingredient information
- Organized categories

## Pain Points

- Lack of trust
- Poor product presentation
- Missing product details

## Buying Motivation

- Natural ingredients
- Sustainability
- Product quality

## Important Features

- Product images
- Detailed descriptions
- Related products

# Persona Summary

| Persona | Primary Goal | Main Pain Point | Most Important Features |
|---------|--------------|-----------------|--------------------------|
| Health-Conscious Parent | Buy healthy food for the family | Difficult navigation | Categories, Search, Checkout |
| Active Lifestyle Customer | Fast healthy shopping | Slow websites | Mobile UX, Search, Product Details |
| Natural Cosmetics Buyer | Find trusted natural cosmetics | Missing information | Images, Descriptions, Categories |

---

# Design Implications

The personas influence the product roadmap in several ways:

- Navigation must remain simple and intuitive.
- Product information should be complete and trustworthy.
- Mobile responsiveness is a priority.
- Search functionality should help users quickly locate products.
- Checkout should require as few steps as possible.
- Product pages should contain clear descriptions and high-quality images.
