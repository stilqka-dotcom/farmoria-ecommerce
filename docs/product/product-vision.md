# Product Vision

---

# Product Overview

Farmoria is an ecommerce platform focused on natural, organic and health-oriented food products. The project combines a modern online shopping experience with cloud-native infrastructure technologies to demonstrate both product management and DevOps capabilities.

The platform is built using WordPress and WooCommerce and is designed to provide customers with a simple, reliable and user-friendly way to discover, compare and purchase natural products.

From a technical perspective, the application demonstrates containerization with Docker, orchestration with Kubernetes and continuous integration through GitHub Actions.

---

# Vision Statement

To build a modern, scalable and reliable ecommerce platform that enables customers to easily discover and purchase trusted organic products while demonstrating best practices in cloud-native application deployment.

---

# Problem Statement

Many online food stores present products in an unstructured way, provide limited filtering capabilities and often deliver a confusing shopping experience.

Customers frequently experience:

- Difficult product discovery
- Poor category organization
- Limited product information
- Slow website performance
- Complicated checkout processes
- Lack of trust in product quality

Farmoria aims to solve these problems by providing a clean, fast and intuitive ecommerce platform.

---

# Target Audience

Farmoria is designed for customers who actively seek healthier lifestyles and higher quality food products.

Primary target groups include:

- Health-conscious families
- Active individuals and athletes
- Customers interested in organic nutrition
- Consumers looking for premium food products
- Returning customers who value convenience

---

# Product Value Proposition

Farmoria delivers value by combining:

- Easy product discovery
- Clean product organization
- Reliable ecommerce functionality
- Fast website performance
- Secure checkout experience
- Modern cloud-native infrastructure
- Scalable deployment architecture

The platform focuses on reducing friction during the purchasing process while maintaining a professional user experience.

---

# Product Goals

Version 1.0 focuses on delivering a complete Minimum Viable Product (MVP).

Primary goals include:

- Functional ecommerce platform
- Product categories
- Product catalog
- Individual product pages
- Shopping cart
- Checkout process
- Mobile-friendly interface
- Search Engine Optimization (SEO) basics
- Reliable Kubernetes deployment
- Continuous Integration pipeline

---

# Success Criteria

Version 1.0 will be considered successful when:

- Customers can browse product categories
- Customers can search for products
- Customers can add products to the cart
- Customers can successfully complete checkout
- Product information is complete and accurate
- The application runs reliably in Kubernetes
- Docker images are automatically built using GitHub Actions
- Documentation fully describes deployment and architecture

---

# Out of Scope (Version 1.0)

The following features are intentionally excluded from Version 1.0:

- Loyalty program
- Customer subscriptions
- AI recommendations
- Advanced analytics
- Multi-language support
- Multi-currency support
- Payment gateway integration
- Public cloud production deployment

These features are planned for future project iterations.
